PRIVACY PLEDGE

POLICY STATUS: ACTIVE LAST REVISED: SEPTEMBER 2026

Your Field Data Stays on Your Hardware

Welcome to TimeSeal, developed by Shankarbros. TimeSeal is engineered from the ground up for field engineers, construction contractors, land surveyors, safety auditors, and logistics inspectors who require legally verifiable, tamper-evident photo and video evidence.

We believe professional field data should never be subjected to unauthorized cloud transmission. TimeSeal operates with a strict zero-cloud surveillance architecture: all photo stamps, video burning, GPS formatting, compass headings, and project notes are calculated and written entirely in local memory on your mobile device.

100% On-Device Execution

No account creation, no logins, and zero remote databases. Captured media never leaves your phone unless you choose to share it.

Direct Hardware Sensors

GPS coordinates, compass azimuth, altitude, and tilt sensors are queried solely to stamp current evidence and are never logged remotely.

No Data Brokerage

We do not collect, sell, lease, or monetize your location history, company project names, or media archives.

02

REQUIRED PERMISSIONS

TimeSeal only requests standard Android runtime permissions that are technically required to capture media, query real-time field coordinates, and save evidence to your gallery.

REQUIRED

Camera Hardware Access

android.permission.CAMERA

Allows the app to access the device's camera hardware to show live viewfinder previews, take high-resolution inspection photos, and record HD video with real-time HUD overlays.

OPTIONAL / PRO

Precise GPS & Location

ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION & ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION

Used strictly to query coordinates (DD Decimal or DMS Survey), elevation/altitude, accuracy tolerances, and geocoded street addresses to print onto the watermark stamp.

REQUIRED

Storage & Media Gallery

READ_MEDIA_IMAGES & READ_MEDIA_VIDEO

Enables TimeSeal to encode and save stamped JPEG photos and MP4 video recordings directly into your device's standard Pictures/TimeStamp and Movies/TimeStamp directories.

HARDWARE

Compass & Gyroscope Sensors

DEVICE HARDWARE SENSORS

Queries device geomagnetic and orientation sensors in real-time to orient the tactical heading needle (degrees & cardinal directions) and calculate pitch/roll tilt verification angles.

03

DATA ARCHITECTURE

Local-Only Processing Pipeline

NO CLOUD TRANSMISSION

All data processed by TimeSeal remains on your device at all times. Here is how your data is handled:

STAMPED MEDIA
Direct Disk Write

Captured images and video frames are processed in system RAM via hardware OpenGL ES shaders and written straight to Android MediaStore.

PROJECT PROFILES
Encrypted Preferences

Job names, contractor tags, custom watermark notes, and coordinate format preferences are stored in local Jetpack DataStore.

EXIF METADATA
Hardware Embedding

GPS latitude, longitude, altitude, and timestamps are embedded directly into standard EXIF headers of the output JPEG files on your device.

Google AdMob Integration

To keep TimeSeal freely accessible for field workers and professionals worldwide, the application displays advertisements powered by Google AdMob. AdMob may automatically collect certain device data according to Google's standard advertising policies:

  • Device Identifiers: Non-sensitive device model, operating system version, and Google Advertising ID (AAID).

  • IP Address: Used for general location estimates (such as country or city level) to display geographically appropriate ads.
