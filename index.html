Privacy Policy

Last Updated: April 6, 2026

Welcome to TimeSeal, developed by Shankarbros. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency about how our app operates. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding your information.

By using TimeSeal, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

🔒

Your Privacy Matters

All your data stays on your device. We don't collect, store, or transmit any personal information to external servers.

🔐

Required Permissions

TimeSeal requires the following permissions to function properly. We only request permissions that are essential for the app's core functionality.

Location Access

Optional

Used to access your current location for the purpose of stamping your media. Location data is never stored or transmitted.

Camera Access

Required

Allows the application to capture photos and videos directly within the app, providing live previews and seamless media stamping.

Storage Access

Required

Enables TimeSeal to access, read, and save your stamped photos and media to your device's storage.

ℹ️

Why These Permissions?

Each permission is essential for TimeSeal to operate. Without these permissions, the app cannot provide its core functionality. We respect your privacy and only use these permissions for their intended purposes.

💾

Data Storage & Privacy

100% Local Storage

All data processed by TimeSeal is stored exclusively on your device. This includes:

  • ✓
    App Configuration

    Your application settings and preferences (like stamp formats and appearance) are saved locally in your device's app data.

  • ✓
    Media Output

    Photos and videos created using the app remain on your device and are never transmitted externally by default.

🛡️

No Data Collection

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information, usage statistics, or file metadata to Shankarbros or any third parties (except as required for advertising - see below).
